Beta — under active development. Expect frequent updates and occasional bugs. Save your projects regularly. Report a problem or suggest a feature. Desktop updates are downloaded manually from GitHub Releases.

1. Upload a CIF, XYZ, MOL (V2000), or SDF (first record).

2. Rotate the model and choose a style. CIF anisotropic Uij/Bij data enable true displacement ellipsoids. Atoms without valid ADPs use spheres.

3. Use Depth fading to soften distant parts of the structure. The same effect is included in PNG, GIF, and WebM exports. Fading blends toward the selected background colour, including for transparent PNGs.

4. Export a PNG, infinitely looping GIF, or WebM video (set your player to repeat).

CIF molecules are completed using the symmetry operators and symmetry-coded bond links stored in the file. Listed bonds are used when available; H bonds are inferred. Full crystal packing is not expanded. Files without symmetry-coded bond tables show deposited coordinates. Check connectivity, particularly metals and disorder. Extended periodic networks are outside the finite-molecule viewer. Alternate occupied sites are displayed as deposited.

Text-based PDF reports can be imported using their atomic coordinate tables. Verify coordinate type, scale, unit cell, and table completeness before rendering. Scans need OCR first. PDF displacement tensors and crystal symmetry are not imported; use a CIF for thermal ellipsoids and symmetry completion. Molecule checkboxes control which connected groups appear in the view and exports; matching connectivity does not establish identical stereochemistry. Exported rotation depicts the static structure rotating, not atomic motion.

No structure or report is uploaded to a server. Save a .crystalstudio project to keep the structure, selected molecules, view, and settings. Keep your original CIF and PDF too. Unsaved browser changes are lost when you close or reload the page.
